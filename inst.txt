# Scan Studies Reference

## CT

### CT Head (Brain), plain
- **Summary:** Fast scan for bleed, stroke, head injury.
- **Also known as:** CT brain, head CT, NCCT head
- **Prep:** None. Remove hairpins, earrings, glasses.
- **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Contrast:** No
- **Report:** Same day
- **Pregnancy:** Tell the radiographer before the scan.

### CT Abdomen and Pelvis with contrast
- **Summary:** Detailed scan of abdomen and pelvis organs. Needs contrast.
- **Also known as:** CT KUB with contrast, CECT abdomen, triple phase CT
- **Prep:**
  - Fasting **6 hours**. Water is allowed.
  - Oral contrast drink: arrive **60 minutes** early.
- **Creatinine report:** Required if age over 60, diabetes, or kidney disease.
- **Contrast allergy:** Do not book. Send to radiologist for review.
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Report:** Same day

### CT Chest (HRCT)
- **Summary:** Detailed lung scan. Used for scarring, infection, nodules.
- **Also known as:** HRCT thorax, CT thorax, lung CT
- **Prep:** None. Patient must hold breath for a few seconds.
- **Contrast:** Usually no. Radiologist decides.
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Report:** Same day

### CT Coronary Angiography
- **Summary:** Heart artery scan with contrast. Heart rate must be low and steady.
- **Also known as:** CTCA, cardiac CT, CT angio heart
- **Prep:**
  - No tea, coffee, cola, or energy drinks for **12 hours**.
  - Fasting **4 hours**.
- **Heart rate:** Below 65 beats per minute. Doctor may give a tablet.
- **Creatinine report:** Required.
- **Duration:** 45 minutes

## MRI

### MRI Brain
- **Summary:** Detailed brain scan. No radiation.
- **Also known as:** MR brain, MRI head
- **Prep:** Remove all metal, cards, phone, hearing aids.
- **Implants:**
  - Pacemaker: **do not book** unless the device card says MR-conditional.
  - Brain aneurysm clip, cochlear implant: send to radiologist first.
- **Claustrophobia:** Ask. Offer open scanner or sedation advice from doctor.
- **Duration:** 30 to 40 minutes
- **Contrast:** Only if the doctor asks.

### MRI Lumbar Spine
- **Summary:** Lower back, discs, and nerves.
- **Also known as:** MRI LS spine, MR lumbar, back MRI
- **Prep:** Remove metal, belts, jewellery.
- **Implants:** Same safety questions as MRI Brain.
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Report:** Next day

### MRI Knee
- **Summary:** Ligament, meniscus, and cartilage injury.
- **Also known as:** MR knee
- **Prep:** Wear loose clothing without metal zips.
- **Duration:** 25 minutes
- **Report:** Next day

### MRI Pelvis
- **Summary:** Uterus, ovaries, prostate, and pelvic bones.
- **Also known as:** MR pelvis, MRI prostate, MRI uterus
- **Prep:** Fasting **4 hours**. Empty bladder 1 hour before.
- **Contrast:** Often needed. Creatinine report required.
- **Duration:** 40 minutes

## X-ray

### X-ray Chest
- **Summary:** Quick lung and heart outline check.
- **Also known as:** CXR, chest PA, chest film
- **Prep:** Remove necklaces and bra with metal hooks.
- **Duration:** 5 minutes
- **Pregnancy:** Tell the radiographer before the scan.
- **Report:** Same day

### X-ray Knee
- **Summary:** Bone injury, arthritis, alignment.
- **Also known as:** Knee AP and lateral
- **Prep:** None. Roll up trousers or change to a gown.
- **Duration:** 10 minutes

### X-ray Lumbar Spine
- **Summary:** Lower back bone alignment and fractures.
- **Also known as:** LS spine X-ray, back X-ray
- **Prep:** Remove belts and metal from clothing.
- **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Pregnancy:** Tell the radiographer before the scan.

## Ultrasound

### Ultrasound Abdomen
- **Summary:** Liver, gallbladder, kidneys, pancreas, spleen.
- **Also known as:** USG abdomen, upper abdomen scan
- **Prep:** Fasting **6 hours**. Water is allowed.
- **Duration:** 20 minutes
- **Report:** Same day

### Ultrasound Pelvis
- **Summary:** Uterus, ovaries, bladder. Full bladder needed.
- **Also known as:** USG pelvis, TAS, transabdominal scan
- **Prep:** Drink **1 litre** of water, finish **1 hour** before. Do not pass urine.
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Note:** Internal (transvaginal) scan needs an empty bladder. The doctor decides.

### Ultrasound Pregnancy (Dating and NT)
- **Summary:** First trimester scan. Checks dating and baby development.
- **Also known as:** NT scan, nuchal translucency, early pregnancy scan, dating scan
- **Timing:** Between **11 weeks and 13 weeks 6 days**.
- **Prep:** Comfortable bladder. Drink water if instructed.
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Bring:** Previous scans and doctor referral.

### Ultrasound Thyroid
- **Summary:** Neck lumps and thyroid size.
- **Also known as:** USG neck, thyroid scan
- **Prep:** None. Remove necklaces.
- **Duration:** 15 minutes

### Doppler Lower Limb (Venous)
- **Summary:** Checks leg veins for clots or weak valves.
- **Also known as:** Venous Doppler, DVT scan, leg Doppler
- **Prep:** None. Wear loose clothing.
- **Duration:** 30 minutes
- **Urgent:** Sudden leg swelling and pain: escalate to supervisor for same-day slot.

## Lab Tests

### Fasting Blood Sugar
- **Summary:** Blood glucose after fasting.
- **Also known as:** FBS, FBG, fasting glucose
- **Prep:** Fasting **8 to 10 hours**. Water is allowed.
- **Sample:** Blood
- **Report:** Same day

### HbA1c
- **Summary:** Average blood sugar over the last 3 months.
- **Also known as:** Glycated haemoglobin, A1c, glycosylated Hb
- **Prep:** None. No fasting needed.
- **Sample:** Blood
- **Report:** Same day

### Lipid Profile
- **Summary:** Cholesterol and triglycerides.
- **Also known as:** Cholesterol test, LDL HDL, lipid panel
- **Prep:** Fasting **10 to 12 hours**. Water is allowed.
- **Sample:** Blood
- **Report:** Same day

### Complete Blood Count
- **Summary:** Red cells, white cells, platelets.
- **Also known as:** CBC, FBC, hemogram, full blood count
- **Prep:** None.
- **Sample:** Blood
- **Report:** Same day

### Thyroid Profile
- **Summary:** Thyroid hormone levels.
- **Also known as:** TSH, T3 T4, TFT, thyroid function test
- **Prep:** None. Tell the lab if the patient takes biotin supplements.
- **Sample:** Blood
- **Report:** Same day

### Vitamin D
- **Summary:** Vitamin D level in blood.
- **Also known as:** 25-OH vitamin D, 25 hydroxy cholecalciferol
- **Prep:** None.
- **Sample:** Blood
- **Report:** Next day

## Doctor

### General Physician Consultation
- **Summary:** Routine health checkups, acute ailments, and general medical evaluation.
- **Also known as:** GP, family doctor, internal medicine
- **Department:** General Medicine
- **Duration:** 15 to 20 minutes
- **Availability:** Mon to Sat, 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM
- **Requirements:** Bring previous medical records and current prescriptions.

### Cardiologist Consultation
- **Summary:** Evaluation for heart health, hypertension, palpitations, and cardiac reviews.
- **Also known as:** Heart specialist, cardiac consultation
- **Department:** Cardiology
- **Duration:** 20 to 30 minutes
- **Availability:** Mon, Wed, Fri, 10:00 AM – 2:00 PM
- **Requirements:** Bring recent ECG, Echo, or blood reports if available.

### Orthopedic Consultation
- **Summary:** Bone, joint, spine, and musculoskeletal injury assessment.
- **Also known as:** Ortho, bone specialist, joint doctor
- **Department:** Orthopedics
- **Duration:** 15 to 20 minutes
- **Availability:** Tue, Thu, Sat, 11:00 AM – 4:00 PM
- **Requirements:** Bring relevant X-ray or MRI scans.

### Radiologist Review
- **Summary:** Scan image review, report interpretation, and clinical protocol guidance.
- **Also known as:** Imaging specialist, scan consultation, second opinion
- **Department:** Radiology & Imaging
- **Duration:** 15 minutes
- **Availability:** Mon to Sat, 8:00 AM – 6:00 PM
- **Requirements:** Scan reference number or DICOM imaging disk.
